Privacy Policy for EDUHUB

App Name: EDUHUB: Code & Learn 0-to-100  |  Package: com.eduhub.android  |  Effective Date: October 24, 2026
Core Privacy Guarantee: EDUHUB is a 100% free, offline-first educational platform. We do not collect, transmit, sell, monetize, or track your personal data. All learning progress and code creations remain strictly on your device.

1. Introduction

EDUHUB ("we", "our", or "the app") respects your privacy. This policy explains our handling of user information in the EDUHUB Android application.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

  • No Personally Identifiable Information (PII): No registration, account creation, real names, emails, phone numbers, or passwords required.
  • No Location Data: We never request or access GPS or network-based location coordinates.
  • No Contacts, Photos, or Private Files: The app does not access your address book, media library, microphone, or external storage.
  • No Ads or Advertising SDKs: Zero advertisements, zero advertising IDs (AAID), and zero marketing trackers.
  • No Behavioral Telemetry: No third-party analytics (e.g. Firebase Analytics, Mixpanel, AppsFlyer) are embedded in this application.

3. Local Storage on Device

All progress and customizations are persisted strictly on your local Android device via an encrypted SQLite Room database:

  • Course progress, completed modules, and syllabus checkpoints across 39 programming tracks
  • User study notes, personal bookmarks, and highlighted cheat sheets
  • Quiz scores, milestone achievements, and XP statistics
  • Offline code snippets drafted inside the interactive Code Playground
  • Generated course graduation certificates

This data stays private to your device. It is only shared if you manually trigger Android's native system Share dialog to share a certificate image or code snippet with a contact.

4. Android Permissions & Justifications

  • android.permission.INTERNET & android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Used only when navigating to external documentation websites or sharing certificates via external apps.
  • android.permission.VIBRATE: Provides light haptic feedback upon submitting quiz answers or completing coding milestones.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR Compliance)

Because EDUHUB collects no personal data whatsoever, it is compliant with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). Students of any age, including children under 13, can use EDUHUB with complete safety.

6. Data Deletion and Retention

You have full autonomy over your data:

  • Clear your data at any time via: Android Settings > Apps > EDUHUB > Storage > Clear Storage / Clear Data.
  • Uninstalling the EDUHUB app immediately removes all stored databases, progress, and certificates from your device.

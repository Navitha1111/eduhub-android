# Privacy Policy for EDUHUB Mobile Application

**Effective Date:** October 24, 2026  
**Last Updated:** October 24, 2026  
**Application Name:** EDUHUB: Code & Learn 0-to-100  
**Package:** `com.eduhub.android`  

---

## 1. Introduction
EDUHUB ("we", "our", or "the app") is committed to respecting and protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding user information when you use the EDUHUB Android application.

Our fundamental principle is simple: **EDUHUB is a 100% free, offline-first educational platform. We do not collect, transmit, sell, or monetize your personal data.**

---

## 2. Information We Do NOT Collect
• **No Personal Identifiable Information (PII):** We do not require registration with real names, emails, phone numbers, or credit cards.
• **No Location Tracking:** We do not access GPS or network location data.
• **No Contacts or Media Files:** We do not read your device contacts, photos, microphone, or external storage.
• **No Third-Party Advertising:** The application contains zero advertisements, zero advertising SDKs, and zero tracking cookies.
• **No Behavioral Analytics or Telemetry:** We do not track your usage, clicks, or keystrokes using analytics SDKs (e.g. Firebase Analytics, Mixpanel, etc.).

---

## 3. Local Data Storage on Device
All app data is stored **locally and exclusively on your Android device** using the Android Room SQLite database:
• Course completion status and lesson progress
• Personal study notes and bookmarks
• Quiz attempts, scores, and XP points
• Custom code snippets created in the Playground
• Generated completion certificates

This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly initiate an external action (such as using the standard Android Share intent to share your certificate or snippet with a friend).

---

## 4. Permissions Requested & Why
• **INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE:** Optional permissions used exclusively when you click external educational links or share content via third-party apps.
• **VIBRATE:** Used for subtle haptic feedback when completing quiz questions or actions.

---

## 5. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR Compliance)
Because EDUHUB does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information from any user, EDUHUB fully complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). Users of all ages, including children under 13, can safely use EDUHUB without privacy risks.

---

## 6. Data Deletion
Since all data is stored locally on your device:
• You can reset all app data at any time via Android **Settings > Apps > EDUHUB > Storage > Clear Data**.
• Uninstalling the app permanently and completely deletes all stored database files, notes, and progress.
